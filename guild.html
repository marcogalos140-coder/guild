Who We Are

About the Guild

The VCSMS Robotics Guild is the official robotics club of Valenzuela City School of Mathematics and Science.

We build, code, and compete — but more than that, we develop future engineers, innovators, and leaders through hands-on STEM learning and collaboration.

About Us

We are a student-led robotics organization dedicated to advancing creativity, engineering, programming, and innovation through practical experiences and competitions.

Our Vision

Through exercising creativity and responsibility, we envision a community that critically forms concepts and creates groundbreaking solutions for real-world problems.

Our Mission

To empower young minds in analyzing findings, designing systems, and creating innovative machines that contribute to technological growth and development.

Our Objectives

1. Encourage innovation and exploration.

2. Cultivate talents to represent the school in robotics-related competitions and activities.

3. Contribute meaningfully to technological advancement in society.

What We Do

We participate in robotics competitions, conduct workshops, organize demonstrations, and collaborate with the Science Department on STEM initiatives and projects.
